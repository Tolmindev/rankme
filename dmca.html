DMCA / Copyright Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026 · RankMe · Ukraine · lolrankme@gmail.com

RankMe respects intellectual property and handles copyright complaints in good faith.

Report Infringement

Email: lolrankme@gmail.com
Subject: Copyright Takedown — [Your Name]

Please include:

  • Description of the copyrighted work
  • Exact URL(s) on RankMe where the material appears
  • Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized agent
  • Signature (typed name is acceptable for email)

What We Do

After a valid complaint we may remove or disable access to the material, contact the affected user where appropriate, and request additional information. Removal is not an admission of wrongdoing.

Counter-Notice

If you believe content was removed in error, email lolrankme@gmail.com with subject Copyright Counter-Notice and include:

  • Identification of the removed content and URL if known
  • Explanation of why removal was a mistake
  • Your contact information
  • A statement that the information is accurate
  • Signature (typed name is fine)

We may forward the counter-notice to the original complainant and may restore content after review if appropriate.

Repeat Infringers

Users who repeatedly violate intellectual property rights may have content removed and accounts suspended or terminated.

Fan-Made Templates & Public Lists

Templates and public tier lists may reference third-party characters, games, franchises, or artwork. All rights remain with their respective owners. Rights holders may request removal of specific materials using the process above.
