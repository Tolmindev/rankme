Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026 · RankMe · Ukraine · lolrankme@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information RankMe collects at https://rankme.lol, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Without an account we may collect IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, and technical logs for security and operation.

Drafts, settings, and images used only in the local builder stay in your browser (localStorage / sessionStorage) and are not uploaded to our servers until you choose to save or publish content that requires storage.

With login (Discord, Google, or X) we may receive account ID, display name, email (if provided), and profile image URL.

Saved and public rankings: if you save Exclusive rankings, publish a public tier list, or store content on RankMe, we may store ranking structure, card order, titles, covers, uploaded images/files you attach, and metadata (including visibility).

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Authenticate users
  • Save rankings and host public tier lists
  • Improve the Service and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Public Content

Content you set as public (including images) may be visible to anyone and may be indexed or shared via links. Do not publish personal data or images you are not allowed to share.

Legal Basis (EU/EEA)

Where GDPR applies: consent; performance of a contract; legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, hosting content you asked us to store); legal obligations.

We Do Not Sell Personal Information

We do not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

Supabase (auth/database/storage), Discord, Google, X (authentication), and hosting/CDN providers. Their own privacy policies apply.

International Transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

Cookies and Local Storage

Used for login sessions, functionality, and preferences. Clearing browser data may log you out or remove local drafts.

Data Retention

Account data and saved/public rankings until deletion or account closure (or removal for legal/policy reasons). Security logs for a limited period. Longer retention only if required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to certain processing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: lolrankme@gmail.com.

Children’s Privacy

Not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). Contact us if a child provided data.

Security

Reasonable measures including HTTPS and access controls. No system is fully secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means acceptance.
